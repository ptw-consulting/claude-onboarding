# Pre-session checklist

For the client. This is the same content as the prep email, in checklist form, in case they want a clean list to work from.

## Have installed

- [ ] Claude desktop app ([Mac](https://claude.ai/download) / [Windows](https://claude.ai/download))
- [ ] Signed in with an active Claude Pro or Max account
- [ ] A cloud storage app already set up on your laptop (iCloud / OneDrive / Drive / Dropbox)

## Logged in (in your browser)

Pick whichever you actually use day to day:

- [ ] Google Workspace — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive
- [ ] **or** Microsoft 365 — Outlook, Calendar, OneDrive
- [ ] Slack (only if you use it)

## Send back to your PTW operator

- [ ] 3-5 writing samples (recent emails or short docs in your voice)
- [ ] Your 1-2 most-hated weekly tasks
- [ ] One "first project" Claude should know about

## Optional, only if easy

- [ ] Short reading list (books recently read or loved)
- [ ] 3-5 personal principles in your own words
- [ ] Anything about role, family, or goals Claude should know
