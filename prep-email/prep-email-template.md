# Prep email template

Send 2-3 days before the in-person session. Edit the bracketed bits.

---

**Subject:** Getting your Claude setup ready — quick prep before [day]

Hi [first name],

Looking forward to getting Claude set up with you on [day] at [time]. To keep us inside 30 minutes, a few things to have ready in advance.

**Have installed:**
- The Claude desktop app — [Mac](https://claude.ai/download) / [Windows](https://claude.ai/download). Sign in with your Pro or Max account.
- A cloud storage app you already use (iCloud, OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox) — we'll back up your Claude memory there so nothing's lost if your laptop dies. No new account needed.

**Be logged in (in your browser) to whichever of these you actually use:**
- Google Workspace (Gmail / Calendar / Drive) **or** Microsoft 365 (Outlook / Calendar / OneDrive)
- Slack

You don't need to install anything for those — just make sure you can open them and you're signed in.

**Send me back, by reply:**
1. 3-5 writing samples — recent emails or short docs that sound like you at your best. Just paste them or forward them. This is what teaches Claude your voice.
2. The 1-2 weekly tasks you most hate doing. Doesn't matter how small. We'll wire Claude to one of them live during the session.
3. One "first project" you'd like Claude to know about (a deal, a client, a recurring piece of work).

**Optional, only if it's easy:**
- A short list of books you've read recently or loved
- 3-5 principles you operate by, in your own words
- Anything about your role, family, or goals you want Claude to know

That's it. See you on [day].

[Signature]
