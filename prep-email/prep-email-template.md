# Prep email template

Send 2-3 days before the in-person session. Edit the bracketed bits.

---

**Subject:** Quick prep before our Claude setup on [day]

[First name],

A few things to have ready before [day] so we don't burn the 30 minutes on installs.

**Have installed:**
- The Claude desktop app -- https://claude.ai/download. Signed in with your Pro or Max account.
- Whatever cloud storage app you already use (iCloud, OneDrive, Drive, or Dropbox). We'll back up your Claude memory there so a dead laptop doesn't lose it. No new account.

**Logged in (in your browser) to whichever you actually use:**
- Google Workspace (Gmail / Calendar / Drive) or Microsoft 365 (Outlook / Calendar / OneDrive)
- Slack -- if it's what you use in your flow

Nothing to install for those -- just signed in and ready to open.

**Sent back to me by reply:**
1. 3-5 writing samples -- recent emails or short docs that sound like you at your best. Paste them in the reply. This teaches Claude your voice.
2. The 1-2 weekly tasks you most hate doing. Doesn't matter how small. We'll wire Claude to one of them live during the session.
3. One first project you'd like Claude to know about (a deal, an active matter, a recurring piece of work).

**Optional, only if easy:**
- A short list of books you've read recently or loved
- A few principles you operate by, in your own words
- Anything about your role, family, or goals you want Claude to know

That's it. See you on [day].

[Signature]
